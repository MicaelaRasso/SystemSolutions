# Canonical certificate template and preserved valve snapshots

**Status: accepted**

The backend will treat `SYS_Certificado Modelo1` as the canonical certificate template, with the System Solutions logo fixed in the upper-left, the client's profile logo in the upper-right when available, and the technician represented by the `Ejecutó` name and signature; no separate provider identity fields are inferred from the reference PDF. Each certificate belongs to one valve and preserves one immutable valve snapshot linked to that valve, so a valve may have multiple historical certificates and snapshots. Valve technical fields may be null when unavailable or inapplicable. The three canonical valve-photo sections are retained even when their images are unavailable. Certificate data may be persisted as a `Certificado pendiente` before signatures are available, while the visit-level signature lifecycle and finalization prerequisites are defined by ADR-0010. The API and Edge Functions persist and expose the complete certificate data, but do not generate or store the PDF; presentation is produced by the consumer. Any Técnico belonging to the Taller Móvil may perform and sign the work. Finalization is allowed only after required data and both visit-level signature images have been stored in a Supabase bucket.
