# Certificate validity and no expiry notifications

**Status: accepted**

A `Certificado finalizado` is valid for one calendar year from its `Fecha de ejecución`, with the validity ending at the end of the anniversary date. A recalibration creates a new certificate and starts a new validity period without changing the immutable historical certificate; the newest unexpired finalized certificate is the current one. Automated expiry notifications are outside the MVP, so Clientes are responsible for monitoring certificate expiration themselves.
