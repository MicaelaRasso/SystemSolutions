# Service requests, visits, and valve work orders

**Status: accepted**

SystemSolutions separates the yacimiento-scoped `Solicitud de servicio`, which grants Taller Móvil access when accepted, from the valve-scoped `Orden de trabajo`, which identifies an individual Válvula to be serviced. Each `Visita de servicio` is scheduled against exactly one Yacimiento with a date and time window, and only an Administrador assigns the Taller Móvil. Simultaneous visits may share a Yacimiento or Planta, but an Equipo cannot belong to more than one simultaneous visit. A Cliente may select work at any level of the asset tree, with upper-level selection including all descendant Válvulas at the time of solicitation; later descendant additions are not included automatically. The Cliente may edit the request until the Taller Móvil accepts the visit, after which the Taller Móvil may add Válvulas as operationally necessary. As an edge case, a Técnico may add a Válvula from another Yacimiento of the same Cliente when the Taller Móvil already has access to it; this does not make the visit a multi-Yacimiento scheduling event. A Taller Móvil rejection returns the visit to `programada` for administrator reassignment, and the Cliente may cancel without administrator approval until the day of execution. Each Orden de trabajo has an independent outcome, so an unevaluated Válvula does not create a certificate or prevent certificates for other Válvulas in the same visit. The Taller Móvil marks the visit complete independently of certificate finalization.
