# Multiple visits per service request

**Status: accepted**

A Solicitud de servicio remains the stable Cliente-facing identity for the requested service and may contain multiple Visitas de servicio. When valves remain without evaluation after a visit, an Administrador regular or Super administrador may create a later visit for the selected valves; that visit creates new Ordenes de trabajo and retains its own date, assignment, signatures, and completion. The earlier Ordenes de trabajo and their outcomes stay with the earlier visit. This keeps each field day operationally independent while the Cliente sees the work under one Solicitud, avoiding both a multi-day Visita and separate Cliente-facing requests.
