# Administrator-led asset registration and Cliente portal

**Status: accepted**

Administradores regulares and Super administradores register and correct Yacimientos, Plantas/locaciones, Equipos/unidades, and Válvulas for a selected Cliente; the Cliente remains the owner of that hierarchy. Administrators also create and schedule the Cliente's service visits. The Cliente-facing portal is intentionally read-oriented: it exposes hierarchy views, certificate signatures, logo upload, and certificate viewing/downloading, while leaving visit scheduling, visit requests, and hierarchy editing out of the frontend. Existing Cliente backend capabilities that are not exposed by the portal are retained for compatibility and possible future use. This lets administrators operate the scheduling workflow while Clientes can use the platform as a support tool rather than as their primary operating mechanism.
